About MISO
MISO is the electric grid operator for the central United States. We ensure power flows reliably and efficiently across 15 states and the Canadian province of Manitoba. Additionally, MISO facilitates the buying and selling of electricity in its region and partners with its stakeholders to plan the grid of the future.

Our Leaders
Our executive team is a dedicated group of professionals and industry leaders with expert knowledge to help MISO reach its vision.

Our Vision
MISO's corporate vision is both simple and transformative. It is to be the most reliable, value-creating Regional Transmission Organization (RTO).

Our History
Since December 2001, MISO's region has expanded to include 15 states and the Canadian province of Manitoba.

MISO continually develops and integrates ESG practices into its business model.
MISO is committed to understanding how environmental, social and governance (ESG) principles impact business, member strategies and the RTO’s reputation.  ESG scores help stakeholders, creditors and investors assess non-financial business factors. While ESG scoring is new to MISO, the concepts of environmental stewardship, social responsibility and strong governance are not.
