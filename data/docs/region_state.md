MISO North: Covers the upper Great Plains and northern Midwest, including Minnesota, Iowa, North Dakota, South Dakota, Montana, and Wisconsin, as well as Manitoba, Canada. This region is a major contributor to the grid's wind energy resources. MISO Central: Encompasses the heart of the Midwest, including Indiana, Illinois, Michigan, Missouri, and parts of Kentucky. MISO's main headquarters is situated within this region in Carmel, Indiana. [1] (https://www.ferc.gov/participation-midcontinent-independent-system-operator-miso-processes)MISO South: Spans the southern footprint, including Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, and parts of eastern Texas. This region officially joined MISO in 2013 when several major southern utilities integrated into the system.
